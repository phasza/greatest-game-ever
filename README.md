# ⚔️ GLITCH DUEL

_Fast 1v1 psychological battle with random chaos_

## Core Loop

- Both players secretly pick an action
- **Optional: Show a fake action to opponent (bluff)**
- 15-second timer
- Reveals happen simultaneously
- Random modifier changes rules each round
- First to 0 HP loses

## Actions

**💣 Attack** - Deal 1 damage, interrupts Heal  
**🛡 Defend** - Blocks Attack & Super  
**⚡ Charge** - Gain 1 Energy (vulnerable to Attack)  
**❤️ Heal** - Gain 1 HP (interrupted by Attack)  
**💀 Super** - Deal 3 damage, costs 2 Energy

## Starting Stats

- 10 HP
- 0 Energy

## 🎭 Bluff System

After selecting your real action:

- **Hide** - Show nothing (default)
- **Reveal** - Show your real action early
- **Bluff** - Show a fake action to psych out opponent

Both players see each other's "shown" actions (or blank if hidden).  
Real actions resolve at the end.

## Chaos Modifiers (1 per round)

Random effects like:

- Double damage
- No defending
- Super costs 1 Energy
- Heal damages instead
- Both lose 1 HP
- Can't repeat last move
- **Must reveal action (no hiding)**
- **All reveals are lies (swapped)**

## Goal

Rock-paper-scissors meets poker meets chaos.  
Pure bluffing, mind games, and adaptation.
